Where things go

What stays on this phone, what leaves over Tor, and what goes direct. Counts are today's, from the network log.

Unlock Onionmind

Your conversation and settings are encrypted on this phone. Enter your password to open them.

Forgot your password?

There is no way to recover it. You can erase the conversation, the network log and the settings and start over. Downloaded models stay.

Settings

These choices apply immediately and stay on this phone. Privacy behavior — Tor and the per-turn search permission — is not a setting.

Send key

Off: Enter makes a newline; the Send button (or Ctrl+Enter) sends. Shift+Enter always makes a newline.

Animation

Reduced keeps progress and pending states static. Follow system honours this phone's animation setting.

Conversation

The conversation stays on this phone and is restored when Onionmind reopens.

Export it to one encrypted file that opens in Onionmind on a computer or another phone, or import one. The file has its own password; without it nobody can open the file.

Encryption

Optional. With a password Onionmind asks for it each time it starts, and nothing on the phone can open the data without it. If you forget it, the data cannot be recovered.

Auto-delete

Counted from the last message. The conversation is deleted the next time Onionmind opens after that.

Network log

Every connection that left this phone while the log was on: time, route (Tor or direct), host and kind. Never what you asked.

Features

New workbench features. Each one shows or hides at once; nothing here changes the Tor boundary.
