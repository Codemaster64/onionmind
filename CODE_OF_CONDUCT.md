# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Onionmind welcomes people who participate in good faith. Be respectful,
specific, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume that users
have different hardware, languages, experience levels, and privacy needs.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
feedback, and other spaces where someone represents the Onionmind project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and whether behavior is
repeated.

## Reporting

For ordinary moderation, flag the content using GitHub's report controls. If a
report contains private or safety-sensitive information, use the repository's
private [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Codemaster64/onionmind/security/advisories/new)
form and begin the report with `Code of Conduct report`. Do not post another
person's private information publicly.
