# Acceptable Use Policy

This software is provided for lawful purposes only.

Users must not use the software to:

- generate, share, or distribute child sexual abuse material;
- incite violence or hatred against individuals or groups;
- promote terrorism or violent extremism;
- provide instructions for serious crimes;
- commit fraud, identity theft, or other criminal offences;
- violate the rights of others, including privacy and intellectual property
  rights; or
- engage in any activity that is illegal under applicable law.

The provider does not and cannot control user behaviour. This policy is not a
technical restriction, but a statement of permitted use.

Nothing in this policy limits liability that cannot be limited under applicable
law, or authorises knowingly facilitating illegal activity.
